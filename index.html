Do I need both proxy and verification code?

Yes. The proxy secures your connection and the verification code confirms your worker identity. Both are required.

Yes! Buy Proxy for KES 700 and Verification Code for KES 800 separately, or get the bundle for KES 1,500 (save KES 100).

After purchasing proxy access, MicroLabs management will send your proxy IP:Port via SMS. Enter it in the unlock form above.
